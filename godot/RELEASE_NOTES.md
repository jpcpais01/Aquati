## Shores O' Fortune for Android and Windows: Hallowtide and the Hollow Jack

The thirty-first build brings the first event ship, for Halloween.

- **Event ships.** Special ships, each one of a kind, sail in for a season
  of the year. While an event is on its ships glow in the harbour's picker
  under an EVENT ribbon; sail one before the season ends and she's yours to
  keep for good.
- **The Hollow Jack (Hallowtide, until 8 November).** A haunted galleon
  captained by a dead man: torn black sails with a burning skull, ghost-fire
  seeping from her seams and gunports, carved pumpkins grinning along her
  rails, skulls on her taffrail, green lanterns astern and a skeleton
  captain on the quarterdeck in his tricorn and coat. Her crew are the
  drowned, pale and glowing, and her cannons fire orange and green
  ghost-fire.
- **Her power: Phantom Run.** Tap the ghost button (G on a computer) and she
  crosses into the spirit world for seven seconds: she thins to a green
  mist, her fires go out, no shot, ram or reef can touch her and she runs
  half as fast again whatever the wind. When she comes back she lets out a
  **Banshee's Wail**, a ring of dread racing over the water that hurts every
  enemy ship it reaches (the nearest worst) and cuts down their gun crews.
  Then the spirits rest for forty seconds before she can call on them again.

### Also from the last build

The thirtieth build brings the web game's fireworks to sea.

- **A sixth kind of shot.** Load fireworks (key 6, or the rocket slice of
  the shot fan on a phone) and a broadside sends rockets up off your guns
  instead of shot: up to four spread along the side, or a showpiece off the
  middle gun with a white salute either side.
- **Thirteen kinds.** Peony, chrysanthemum, weeping willow, island palm,
  halo rings, crossettes, lighthouse strobes, dragon's eggs, a shoal of
  fish and Saturn, plus three rare showpieces: Kraken's Bloom, the Jolly
  Roger written in white fire, and Fortune's Finale.
- **They light the night.** Each burst lights the sails, decks and islands
  round it, shimmers on the sea under it and leaves smoke hanging in the
  sky, with its own bangs, whistles, crackles and sizzles.
- **A firework locker.** Rockets are kept in a locker that follows you
  from voyage to voyage. Pick the kind from the locker (6 again, or a click
  on the rocket slot), buy more from the pyrotechnist at any trading port
  (a different counter every day), and find them in fine chests. Every
  captain starts with a small welcome crate.
