## Shores O' Fortune for Android: updates from the harbour

The twentieth build is the last one you'll need to install by hand. From
now on the game keeps itself up to date: every new build is offered in
the harbour, downloads inside the game and installs with a tap.

- **"A new build has made port."** When a newer build is out, a card comes
  up over the harbour: the crest turning in its gold halo, the new version
  in gilt beside yours, and everything the build brings, its notes to
  scroll through, with any builds you skipped named too.
- **Update, right there.** Tap Update (it tells you the download's size)
  and your ship sails a gilt bar as it comes in, with how much has come,
  how far it's got and how long it has left. Close the card and it carries
  on: the harbour's Update pill counts it up, then says Install.
- **Checked before it's opened.** The download is checked against the seal
  GitHub keeps for it, then handed to Android's installer. The first time,
  Android asks you to allow installs from Shores O' Fortune; allow it,
  come back and tap Install. Your ships, treasure and progress all stay
  aboard.
- **Never in the way.** Offline, it simply says nothing; a download that
  breaks off can be tried again, and a finished one waits for you even if
  you close the game.
- **Settings → Game updates** shows your version and looks for a newer one
  whenever you like. With nothing newer out, it shows what's in your build.
