## Shores O' Fortune for Android: the crew on deck

**New in this update: the shot pie.** Hold the shot button over Fire and a
pie of eight slices opens round it, the five kinds of shot across its top
with what's left of each. Slide your thumb onto one and let go to load it,
or back to the middle to keep what you have. The slice under your thumb
lights up in its shot's colour, with its name and what it does above the
pie, and the guns dim while you choose. A quick tap opens the pie too, for
a tap on the shot you want.

The sixth build puts people aboard. Every ship on the sea, yours and every
pirate's, now sails with a crew at work on her deck, each sailor with his
own face and kit, and they fight, cheer, panic and swim just as they do on
the web.

- **Hands on deck.** A helmsman at the wheel, the captain pacing the
  quarterdeck and stopping for a long look through his spyglass, and deck
  hands at their chores: hauling on the lines when you trim the sails or
  come about, swabbing the deck, coiling rope, leaning on the rail, sitting
  on crates and capstans, chatting, smoking a pipe or sipping grog. Now and
  then a fiddler strikes up a tune and the hands nearby dance a jig.
- **Every sailor his own.** Each one is rolled with his own skin, hair and
  clothes: linen shirts and sea blues on your ship, black and blood red on
  the pirates', striped shirts and sashes, bandanas (half of them spotted),
  knit caps and straw hats, beards and moustaches, ponytails, eye patches,
  earrings, vests, boots, bare feet and the odd peg leg. Every captain wears
  a coat and a plumed tricorn, and most keep a parrot on their shoulder.
- **Battle stations.** When a fight starts the gunners run to the guns on
  the side that bears and work them, powder monkeys shuttle cartridges up
  from the hatch, and the captain takes the rail on the engaged side and
  bellows the order as each broadside goes. Everyone ducks when a ball
  strikes home and roars when an enemy goes down, and the hands run for
  buckets when the ship catches fire.
- **Abandon ship.** When a ship goes down her helmsman fights the wheel to
  the last, her captain stands to salute and goes down with her, and the
  rest scramble for the high side and leap over the rail to swim among the
  wreckage.
- **Made for phones.** Every sailor on the sea is posed on the graphics
  chip and drawn in a handful of instanced draws: the full sculpt only for
  the few close to the lens, simpler cuts further off and a tiny figure
  once a sailor is a few pixels tall. Crews out of sight think less often,
  and phones on the lower quality settings carry one deck hand fewer per
  ship.

Still aboard from the earlier builds: the open sea with its swells, sun
glitter and turning day, both archipelagos with their islands, plants,
shallows and surf, the pirates and their broadsides, five kinds of shot,
holes, broken masts, wrecks breaking in two and wreckage afloat, plunder
and your hold, and your farewell when the sea takes you.

New in this release: the launcher icon is now Fair Winds, a toy galleon
heeling through a bright blue swell, framed so launchers that crop icons
into circles keep her masts and bowsprit in view.
