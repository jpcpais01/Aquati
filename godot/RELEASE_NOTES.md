## Shores O' Fortune for Android: shot and splinters

The fourth build makes every hit count where you can see it. Ships now
carry the scars of a fight, lose their masts, break in two as they go
down, and leave the sea strewn with wreckage.

- **Holes you can see.** Every ball tears a jagged breach in her planking,
  ringed with raw splintered wood, soot and embers that cool off, with
  broken planks bristling out of it. Through the bigger breaches you can see
  into her dark hold. Scorch spreads over her hull as the fight goes on and
  her sails tear into ragged, burnt-edged rags. When the guns fall quiet her
  crew boards the breaches over with fresh planks, one by one.
- **Masts that come down.** A ball through a mast, or a beating that leaves
  her badly hurt, snaps it off with a crack: it topples over the side with
  its yards, sails and flag, crashes into the sea and floats away, and the
  ropes made fast to it go with it. The bowsprit can be shot away too. A
  ship that has lost a mast is slower. Once she's sound again her crew
  re-rigs her, hauling each spar back up out of its stump in a shower of
  sawdust.
- **Wrecks that break in two.** When a ship goes, she either breaks her
  back, the two halves of her hull sagging apart round a jagged, glowing
  break, or rolls over onto her beam ends. Either way her masts come down,
  her lanterns and cabin windows go dark, and her cargo spills into the sea.
- **Wreckage afloat.** Planks blasted off by every hit tumble through the
  air and slap into the water, barrels and crates are thrown off a dying
  ship, and the spars she loses float off in the swell. It all bobs and
  turns on the waves, drifts with the wind and slowly waterlogs and sinks,
  and hulls shoulder floating junk aside as they sail through it. When your
  own ship goes under she tears loose timber that floats up past her.
- **Made for phones.** Floating wreckage is moved by your phone's graphics
  chip on the very swell the sea draws, so a sea full of planks costs almost
  nothing; a ship that has never been hit draws exactly as cheaply as
  before; and masts are cut where they can break when the ship is built, so
  nothing has to be sliced up in the middle of a fight.

Still aboard from the earlier builds: the open sea with its swells, sun
glitter and turning day, both archipelagos with their islands, plants,
shallows and surf, and the pirates, the broadsides and your farewell when
the sea takes you.
