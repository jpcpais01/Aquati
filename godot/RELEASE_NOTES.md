## Shores O' Fortune for Android and Windows: now on Windows

The twenty-third build sails onto the PC. Every release now comes as a
Windows game as well as the Android one, built from the same code and
looking just the same.

- **One file, nothing to install.** Download the `.exe`, run it and you're
  in the harbour. Windows may say it protected your PC from an unrecognised
  app the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway.
- **Mouse and keys.** A and D steer, W and S set more or less sail, Q and E
  fire each broadside and Space whichever side bears, and a click on the sea
  aims at what you point at. 1 to 5 load a kind of shot, M opens the sea
  chart, F comes alongside a quay, C changes the camera and the wheel zooms.
  Your first voyage shows the keys, and Settings lists them all.
- **Full screen or a window.** It opens full screen; F11 or Alt+Enter (or
  the new Full screen switch in Settings) puts it in a window you can size
  as you like, and it remembers which you chose. Settings also has Quit to
  desktop.
- **It tells you when there's a newer one.** The harbour's Update card works
  on Windows too: it shows what the new build brings, and Download fetches it
  in your browser. Your ships and treasure live in your user folder, so the
  new build carries on right where you left off.
