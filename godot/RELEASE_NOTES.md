## Shores O' Fortune for Android: the helmsman

The thirteenth build puts a helmsman at the wheel: pick a place on the sea
chart, press "Sail there" and he takes her there himself.

- **Pick a place.** Tap a waypoint, a market town or an island on the sea
  chart and a card opens over it: its name, how far it is and roughly how
  long she'll take, with a word when she'll have to beat upwind (and a
  reminder that charting an unexplored isle pays a bounty). Tap open sea and
  a pin drops there with its card, where you can sail to it or lift it.
- **Sail there.** The helmsman plots a course round the islands, the living
  coral and every shoal too shallow for your keel, and the course glows on
  the chart and the minimap as a thread of gold flowing toward your goal. A
  moment later the chart rolls itself up so you can watch her go (unless your
  hand is still on it).
- **He sails like a seaman.** He works the wind: when the course runs into
  its eye he beats up it in long tacks, coming about before the clear water
  runs out, far faster than pinching straight into it. He lays off for the
  current, gives way to ships under your bow, shortens sail into sharp turns
  and eases in so she glides to a stop on her mark. At a market town he
  brings her up off the quay with the prompt to come alongside waiting; at an
  island he lies her off the shore that faces you.
- **The helm pill.** Low on the screen while he has the wheel: where she's
  bound, the distance and time left, how she's sailing (on a broad reach,
  beating to windward, coming about, easing in), a ship's wheel that turns as
  he steers and a gold thread that fills as the course falls behind her. It
  flares as she arrives.
- **Take the helm back** whenever you like: steer yourself, tap the cross on
  the pill, or "Stop sailing" on the card. Raising or lowering sail sets the
  canvas he cruises under, and furling it all stops her.

Still aboard from the earlier builds: the minimap and the sea chart with your
waypoints, the harbour where you choose your ship, "Where shall we sail?",
the pause screen and Settings, your progress kept between plays, the trading
ports with their markets and chandleries, the open sea with its swells, sun
glitter and turning day, the weather (eight skies, storms with lightning,
rain, mist and mirror calm), the night with its stars, moon, lanterns and
glowing seas, both archipelagos with their islands, plants, shallows and
surf, the pirates and their broadsides, five kinds of shot and the shot pie,
holes, broken masts, wrecks breaking in two and wreckage afloat, plunder and
your hold, the crews on deck, every sound of the sea and the band's songs,
and your farewell when the sea takes you.
