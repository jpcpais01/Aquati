## Shores O' Fortune for Android and Windows: made for the PC

The twenty-fifth build makes the Windows game feel at home on a computer.

- **The right size on every screen.** The menus and the HUD were sized for
  a phone, so on a monitor everything came out twice as big as it should.
  They're now sized the way the web game sizes them on a computer, for
  your window and your display's scaling: crisp and in proportion at 1080p,
  1440p and 4K, on ultrawides and in small windows. Settings has a new
  Interface size (Compact, Normal, Large) if you like it bigger or smaller.
- **Updates from inside the game.** When a new build is out, the harbour's
  card shows what it brings; Update downloads it right there, your ship
  sailing a gilt bar with the size, speed and time left, and checks it
  against its seal. Restart & update swaps it in and opens the new build
  straight away, with everything you've won.
- **Exit game.** A new button by Settings in the harbour, and Quit game at
  the foot of the pause menu (it asks for a second press, so a stray click
  never ends a voyage).
