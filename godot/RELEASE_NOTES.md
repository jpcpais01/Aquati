## Shores O' Fortune for Android: weather

**New in this update: a smooth wake.** The wake behind your ship no longer
stutters. Several times a second the whole wake, its foam and its shine
used to hop back and forth by half a metre; now the bow waves, the trail
of foam and their reflections glide smoothly astern.

The seventh build brings the weather. The sky no longer stays fair: it
turns as you sail, just as it does on the web, and every kind of weather
changes the light, the wind and the sea.

- **Eight skies.** Clear, fair, cloudy, sea mist, rain, storm, mirror calm
  and a scorching noon follow one another on their own, each with its own
  light, clouds and wind. A note tells you when the weather turns, and the
  wind backs and veers with it.
- **Storms.** The sky closes into a dark slate deck and the swell builds.
  Whitecaps break along the crests, the gale tears the tops off them in
  sheets of spray, and foam streams away in long streaks down the wind.
  Lightning forks down to the sea, lighting the clouds from inside, the
  water round the strike and, for an instant, the whole world.
- **Rain.** It drives across the water in drifting curtains, rings spread
  where the drops land, and decks, rocks and sails go dark and glossy as
  they soak, drying slowly after.
- **Sea mist.** Banks of mist lie low on the water with clear gaps between
  them, drifting by on the wind.
- **Mirror calm.** The wind dies and the sea turns to glass, mirroring the
  sky and its clouds.
- **Very hot.** Out on the high seas at noon the horizon bleaches to a
  white glare and a thin haze shimmers on the water.
- **Clouds.** Their shadows drift across the sea, the islands and the
  ships, and the sea mirrors them.
- **Pick your sky.** Tap the weather badge at the top right to hold a
  weather, or the clock beside it to hold an hour (dawn, noon, golden hour,
  dusk or night), and tap on round to Auto to let the sky run free again.
  Your picks are kept for the next voyage.
- **The high seas.** Out between the archipelagos the open ocean keeps its
  own weather: a fair trade wind and a long, easy swell.
- **Made for phones.** The rain, the spray and the lightning are each one
  draw, built once and animated entirely on the graphics chip, and none of
  it is drawn under a fair sky.

Still aboard from the earlier builds: the open sea with its swells, sun
glitter and turning day, both archipelagos with their islands, plants,
shallows and surf, the pirates and their broadsides, five kinds of shot and
the shot pie, holes, broken masts, wrecks breaking in two and wreckage
afloat, plunder and your hold, the crews on deck, and your farewell when the
sea takes you.
