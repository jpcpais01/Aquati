## Shores O' Fortune for Android: shot and plunder

The fifth build fills your racks and your hold. Your guns can now load five
kinds of shot, each with its own look in flight and its own way of hurting
a ship, and every pirate you sink leaves her plunder floating for you to
haul aboard.

- **Five kinds of shot.** Round shot is the honest all-rounder and never
  runs out. Explosive shells sputter on their fuses and burst on impact,
  hurting every ship close by, even on a near miss. Red-hot fire shot trails
  embers and sooty smoke and sets a ship ablaze, burning her down until the
  fire dies. Chain shot cartwheels through the air on its chain and shreds
  sails, slowing a ship to a crawl. Grape shot sprays a deck with musket
  balls and cuts down the gun crews, so they reload slowly. The specials
  come in short supply. Pirates load them too, now and then.
- **Picking your shot.** The round button beside Fire shows what's loaded
  and how much of it is left. Tap it to fan out all five kinds with their
  counts, then tap one to ram it home. With a keyboard, keys 1 to 5 load a
  kind and R cycles through them.
- **Ships that burn, tear and bleed.** A ship set ablaze, with her canvas
  in rags or her crews cut down shows it with a little flame, torn sail or
  crossed-out sailor on her health bar, and words rise off her as it
  happens.
- **Plunder afloat.** Sunken ships leave barrels and treasure chests
  bobbing on the swell: commons, uncommons, rares, epics and legendaries,
  each with its own chest and a beacon on the water in its rarity's colour,
  the finer ones with a shaft of light you can spot from afar. Sail through
  them to haul them in. Each patches a little of your hull, tops up your
  special shot and stows its goods, and a fine find gets its own card and
  fanfare.
- **The ship's hold.** A crate on the HUD shows how full your hold is. Tap
  it for a look inside: sixty kinds of goods, from casks of rum to the Crown
  of the Drowned King, each with its own little picture, finest first, and
  roughly what it would all fetch at market. When the hold is full, the
  cheapest goods go over the side to make room for better ones. (Trading
  ports, where you sell it all, come in a later build.)
- **Made for phones.** Every ball still flies on your phone's graphics chip
  from a single write when it's fired, special shot included. Floating
  plunder rides the swell the sea draws there too, and a whole rarity of
  chests and barrels costs a single draw.

Still aboard from the earlier builds: the open sea with its swells, sun
glitter and turning day, both archipelagos with their islands, plants,
shallows and surf, the pirates and their broadsides, holes, broken masts,
wrecks breaking in two and wreckage afloat, and your farewell when the sea
takes you.
