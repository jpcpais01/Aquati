## Shores O' Fortune for Android: updates from the harbour

The twenty-first build is the last one you'll need to install by hand. From
now on the game keeps itself up to date: every new build is offered in
the harbour, downloads inside the game and installs with a tap.

- **"A new build has made port."** When a newer build is out, a card comes
  up over the harbour: the crest turning in its gold halo, the new version
  in gilt beside yours, and everything the build brings, its notes to
  scroll through, with any builds you skipped named too.
- **Update, right there.** Tap Update (it tells you the download's size)
  and your ship sails a gilt bar as it comes in, with how much has come,
  how far it's got and how long it has left. Close the card and it carries
  on: the harbour's Update pill counts it up, then says Install.
- **Checked before it's opened.** The download is checked against the seal
  GitHub keeps for it, then handed to Android's installer. The first time,
  Android asks you to allow installs from Shores O' Fortune; allow it,
  come back and tap Install. Your ships, treasure and progress all stay
  aboard.
- **Never in the way.** Offline, it simply says nothing; a download that
  breaks off can be tried again, and a finished one waits for you even if
  you close the game.
- **Settings → Game updates** shows your version and looks for a newer one
  whenever you like. With nothing newer out, it shows what's in your build.

### Also from the last build: going ashore

Build twenty put your feet on the sand. Off any beach you can lower
the boat and row the captain and four hands ashore, then walk the islands.

- **Go ashore.** Come in close to a beach and a pill offers to row ashore
  (F, or tap it). Your ship rides at anchor while the boat is pulled up on
  the sand. Not while pirates are on your tail, or with a maelstrom pulling.
- **Your own crew.** The captain and four hands step off her deck, with the
  same faces and clothes, and walk with a real stride: heel strike, swaying
  hips, swinging arms, a run that leaves the ground, high steps through the
  shallows.
- **A living landing party.** The hands keep a loose knot behind you, each
  on his own line, setting off a beat late, stopping to look at something
  and jogging to catch up, chatting with a mate as they go. Stand still and
  they watch the sea, lean, chat, and in the end sit down on the sand.
- **On foot.** The camera drops low over the captain's shoulder. Walk with
  WASD or the arrows (Shift runs, Space jumps), or on a phone with the stick
  under your left thumb (push it to the rim to run) and the Jump button.
  Trees, boulders, cliffs, deep water and the towns' walls stop you; sand
  puffs up at a run and the shallows splash.
- **Row back** from the boat (F, or tap the pill) and she weighs anchor.

Cutthroat camps, sword fights, buried treasure and forts come next.
