## Shores O' Fortune for Android: the maps

The twelfth build hands you the charts: a minimap in the corner, the whole
sea chart, and waypoints you can drop anywhere on the sea.

- **The minimap.** Top right, under the clock: the waters round you engraved
  in ink and colour, your ship at its heart, the pirates within spyglass
  range, plunder afloat, the market towns and your waypoints, under a gilt
  bezel with north marked on it. The corner column round it now looks just
  as it does in the web game.
- **The sea chart.** Tap the minimap (or press M) and the whole known sea
  unrolls over the world, which keeps sailing behind frosted glass. The open
  sea is engraved by its depths: shallow teal shelves falling away to the
  abyss, rolling banks, the long shoal under the sailing lane, a leadsman's
  soundings in fathoms, the rhumb lines of a great compass rose and the
  Sereno current running either side of the lane. Each archipelago is
  engraved from the very seabed you sail over, its isles named once you've
  charted them; waters you've never sailed into lie under mist, known only
  from sailors' tales.
- **Live on the chart.** Your ship and her heading, the pirates within
  spyglass range, plunder afloat, the market towns, the wind on its own
  little rose and a scale bar that keeps up with the zoom.
- **Drag, pinch, double tap.** Pan and zoom from the whole sea down to a
  single cove, and the centre button brings you back over your ship.
- **Waypoints.** Tap open sea on the chart to drop a numbered pin there, up
  to five, each in its own colour, and tap a pin to lift it again. Every pin
  rises in the world as a pillar of light with ripples where it meets the
  sea, the chart draws a dotted course to it with how far it is, and sailing
  into one strikes it off in a flare. "Clear pins" lifts them all at once, and
  your pins are kept between plays.

Still aboard from the earlier builds: the harbour where you choose your ship,
"Where shall we sail?", the pause screen and Settings, your progress kept
between plays, the trading ports with their markets and chandleries, the open
sea with its swells, sun glitter and turning day, the weather (eight skies,
storms with lightning, rain, mist and mirror calm), the night with its stars,
moon, lanterns and glowing seas, both archipelagos with their islands, plants,
shallows and surf, the pirates and their broadsides, five kinds of shot and
the shot pie, holes, broken masts, wrecks breaking in two and wreckage afloat,
plunder and your hold, the crews on deck, every sound of the sea and the
band's songs, and your farewell when the sea takes you.
