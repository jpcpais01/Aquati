    Ashore

    FOff a beach: row the captain and four hands ashore
    WASDWalk (Shift to run)
    SpaceCutlass: chain three cuts, the last a lunge
    ClickCut toward where you point
    QEFire your pistol (a slow reload)
    FOpen a chest, dig at the X, or row back by the boat
    TUnroll your treasure map
    Cutthroats camp on many isles. Get close and they come running: your hands pick a man each and rush to anyone going for you. Rout the camp and its chest is yours.
    Camps and sea chests hold torn pieces of treasure maps. Find all three, sail to the isle with the X, and dig where the crossed sticks lie.
    Board a beaten pirate and her crew can sign on with you, or hire a ship from a harbour master. Up to two escorts sail on your quarters and fight at your side.
    Sail across the wind for the best speed. Heading straight into it is slow going.
